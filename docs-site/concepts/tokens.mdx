---

Kassandra does **not** have a native protocol token. Stakes, bonds, market
funding, and in-app fees are denominated in **SOL** (wrapped SOL, 9 decimals)
or **USDC** (6 decimals). The oracle's dispute path is backed by a trusted
external AI feed plus decision markets — a custom token is not required to
keep that honest.

## SOL — stakes, collateral, and fees

Wrapped SOL is pinned as the canonical `base_mint` on the `Protocol` singleton
and snapshotted onto every `Oracle` and market. Every SOL-denominated flow
touches the oracle's `stake_vault` or a market's escrow.

Conditional tokens (cYES/cNO) are minted from SOL via the MetaDAO
conditional-vault CPI and redeem 1:1 for SOL on the winning side.

## USDC — challenge-market quote

USDC is the external stable collateral used in the challenge phase (and as the
quote side of the pass/fail AMMs). A challenger opens a decision market by
posting USDC, sized to the proposer's bond at the current SOL/USDC spot TWAP:

`required_usdc = bond * spot_price / SPOT_PRICE_SCALE`

(`spot_price` is the governance-anchored futarchy TWAP; `SPOT_PRICE_SCALE = 1e12`).
At settlement:

- **Challenge succeeds (disqualify):** the full USDC escrow returns to the challenger; a SOL fee from the bond goes to the challenger.
- **Challenge fails (survive):** a small `usdc_fee` (default 1/100) goes to the proposer; the remainder returns to the challenger.

The USDC mint is pinned per oracle as `usdc_mint`.

## What was removed

There is no KASS (or any other) native token, no program mint authority, and no
participation emissions. Supply of SOL/USDC is outside this protocol. Rewards
to honest participants still come from **slashed counterparties** (the same
bond-pool / claim split as before).
